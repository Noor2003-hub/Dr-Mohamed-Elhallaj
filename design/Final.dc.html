Who is Dr. Elhallaj?

My fascination with the human body made me want to become a physiotherapist,

along with a wish to help children with cerebral palsy and high-level disabilities.

I have always been interested in how the body works and repairs itself,

and how exercise and hydrotherapy can improve it.

Achedaway Cupping Therapy

We use Achedaway Cupping Therapy to help relieve muscle tension, reduce discomfort, and improve local blood circulation through controlled suction. It is used as part of a recovery program to promote muscle relaxation and support overall mobility.
